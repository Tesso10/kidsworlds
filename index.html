Store Policies

Shop with confidence

These policies apply in our stores. Please keep your receipt.

Returns & Exchanges

Not completely satisfied? Bring the item back with your receipt.

  • Exchanges within 30 days with receipt
  • Refunds within 7 days with receipt
  • No refunds or exchanges without a receipt

Sale Items

Sale items can be exchanged, but not refunded.

  • Exchange only, within 30 days with receipt
  • No refunds on sale items

Layaway Plan

We gladly hold your selection for 30 days.

  • Deposit of at least 25% of the layaway total (minimum $10)
  • Complete your payments within 30 days
  • $5 layaway fee · $8 cancellation fee
  • Sale and clearance items not eligible
  • No cash refund — store credit only

Payment

Cash and credit cards accepted.

  • Picture ID required for all credit card purchases
  • Personal checks not accepted

Questions about a purchase? Call your store, receipt in hand.
